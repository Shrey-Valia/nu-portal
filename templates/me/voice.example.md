# Voice

How your writing should sound. The humanizer uses this on every cover letter
and written answer.

- Direct and specific. Lead with what I built or did, then why it matters to them.
- Short paragraphs. Under 300 words for a cover letter.
- Plain words. No "passionate", "leverage", "synergy", "I am excited to apply".
- One concrete detail about the company that shows I read the posting.
- Sign off with "Best," and my first name.
