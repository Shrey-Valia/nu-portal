# Stories

Short STAR stories (situation, task, action, result) NU Portal can draw on for
cover letters and "tell us about a time…" questions. Keep the heading format:
`## [story:some-id] Title`. Only true details; the AI may not add facts.

## [story:tracker-launch] Shipping my co-op tracker

- Situation: Applying to co-ops meant tracking 60+ postings in a spreadsheet.
- Task: Make something that kept track for me and cut repetitive work.
- Action: Built a TypeScript + Playwright tool with a SQLite store and a daily report.
- Result: Used it for my whole search; three friends started using it too.
